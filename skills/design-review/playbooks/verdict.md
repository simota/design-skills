# Verdict

The overall judgement, and how to say it so it can be argued with.

## The verdict is one sentence

Not a list. The list is what `design-critique` produces; this is the thing a
list cannot say — whether the interface holds together, and how far off it is.

| Verdict | Means | What follows |
|---|---|---|
| `holds` | It reads as designed. Weaknesses are local | Name them, and stop |
| `holds, thin` | Nothing is wrong and nothing is chosen. It reads as a competent default | The gap is direction, not execution |
| `uneven` | Parts are strong, parts are not, and they do not read as one hand | Name which parts, and which one to bring the others to |
| `does not hold` | The whole misreads, whatever the parts do | Say what the whole is trying to be. Line-level fixes are wasted here |

**Say the verdict before the evidence.** A reader who has to reach paragraph
nine to learn whether it is fine has been told nothing by paragraph eight.

## Grounding it

Each verdict carries the three phases, or names the one it is missing.

| Phase | Present when | Absent means |
|---|---|---|
| Impression | The conditions and the first reading are recorded | There was no looking. Nothing else survives |
| Principle | The impression restates as one named principle with evidence | It is a feeling. Legitimate, and labelled `ARBITRARY` |
| Reference | An opened comparison, cited | The claim is that it is weak, not that better is possible |

Impression plus principle is a usable verdict. All three is a strong one.
Reference alone is envy, and principle alone is an audit nobody felt.

## Phrasing

- **Describe, then judge.** "The primary action is the third-heaviest element on
  the screen, so the eye reaches it last" beats "the hierarchy is bad"
- **Say the strengths with the same specificity.** "It looks nice overall" is
  the same non-statement as "it looks off", and it is the half that gets deleted
  in the next revision because nobody knew it was load bearing
- **Own the subjectivity where it exists.** "This is taste: I would want more
  contrast between the surfaces, and nothing fixes that number" is honest,
  actionable, and impossible to mistake for a defect
- **Never scold.** A verdict that reads as an attack is discussed rather than
  acted on, and the discussion is about the tone

## What this never becomes

- **A redesign.** The moment a fix is specified in values, it belongs to the
  skill that owns those values
- **A ranking of products.** The reference is a tool for locating the gap, not a
  standard the product agreed to meet
- **A score.** A number implies a scale nobody defined, and it survives long
  after the reasoning under it is forgotten
